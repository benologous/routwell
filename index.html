Routwell: Meal & Pill Reminder

Healthy habits that fit your day. Routwell builds one simple daily timeline and gently reminds you when to eat, take your medicine, move, drink water and wind down, adjusting around what you have already done and what is on your calendar.

  • Meal, medicine, workout, water and bedtime reminders
  • Medicine reminders that repeat until you tap Taken or Skip
  • Reminders that move around your meetings, with one-tap Undo
  • Your routine, medicines and health information stay on your iPhone

Routwell is for adults aged 18 and over. It is a reminder tool, not a medical device, and it never suggests or changes doses. Follow your healthcare provider's instructions.

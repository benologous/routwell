Routwell Privacy Policy

Effective date: 9 November 2026 (updated for worldwide availability)

Who we are: Benjamin Usulor, an individual developer based in the United Kingdom, is the controller of the personal data described in this policy. Contact: Bentech391@gmail.com.

The short version

Your routine, medicines, check-ins and health information stay on your iPhone. Routwell has no accounts and we do not run servers that receive that information. Routwell shows no ads. To measure our own advertising, and only with your permission, the app shares anonymous install and purchase information with a measurement provider (AppsFlyer). We never share health, medicine, routine, name or calendar information, and we do not sell your information.

What Routwell stores on your iPhone

  • Your first name, routine times (wake, meals, workout, bedtime) and reminder preferences.
  • Medicine names, doses and times exactly as you type them, and whether you marked a dose taken or skipped.
  • Water and meal check-ins you log.
  • Your answers to optional safety questions (for example diabetes, pregnancy, history of disordered eating). These only adjust what the app shows.

This information stays in the app's private storage on your device. It is not sent to us.

Permissions you can choose to give

  • Notifications: to deliver reminders. They are created and shown by your iPhone.
  • Apple Health (read only): steps and workouts, so a reminder can be skipped when you are already on track. Routwell never writes to Health, never stores Health data on a server, never shares it, and never uses it for advertising or marketing.
  • Calendar (read only): the start and end time of your events, so reminders can move around meetings. Event titles, locations and attendees are not read or stored.

Routwell works without any of these. You can change them at any time in iPhone Settings.

Purchases

Subscriptions are handled by Apple. We do not receive your payment details. Apple tells the app whether you have an active subscription.

Advertising measurement (AppsFlyer)

We advertise Routwell on third-party platforms. To learn which ads lead to installs and purchases we use a measurement provider, AppsFlyer (see AppsFlyer's privacy policy).

  • What is shared: that the app was installed or opened, that onboarding was completed, that the paywall was viewed, and the product, price and currency when you subscribe, together with technical identifiers such as an advertising identifier (only if you allow tracking in Apple's prompt), your device and app version, and the IP address your phone connects from.
  • What is never shared: anything you enter in the app (name, routine, medicines, doses, check-ins, safety answers), anything from Apple Health, calendar information, or any health-related event. Health data is never used for advertising or marketing.
  • Your choices: choose “Ask App Not to Track” in Apple's prompt, tap “Skip for now”, or switch off Me → Privacy → Help measure our ads at any time. If you decline tracking, the app uses anonymous mode (no advertising identifier). You can also change tracking in iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Legal basis (UK GDPR / PECR): your consent, given through Apple's tracking prompt and our in-app screen, which you can withdraw at any time.
  • International transfers: AppsFlyer is a service provider acting on our instructions and may process data outside the UK under appropriate safeguards described in its own policy.

Legal basis, retention and transfers

Legal basis and consent by region. In the UK, EEA and similar regions we rely on your consent for advertising measurement. In the United States and other regions, we rely on your choice through Apple's tracking prompt and our in-app switch. Declining or withdrawing has no effect on how the app's reminders work.

Retention. Information on your iPhone stays until you delete it or remove the app. Advertising-measurement records are kept by AppsFlyer under its own retention schedule and our instructions, and we do not combine them with anything you enter in the app. Emails you send us are kept only as long as needed to answer you and keep a record of your request, then deleted.

International transfers. We are based in the United Kingdom. Our measurement provider may process data in other countries, including the United States, the European Union and Israel. Where the law requires it, transfers rely on adequacy decisions or standard contractual clauses described in the provider's own policy.

What we do not do

No ads inside the app. No sale of personal information. No sharing of health, medicine, routine or calendar information with anyone. No analytics or crash-reporting tools other than the advertising measurement described above.

Backups

Your iPhone may include app data in your own iCloud or computer backup. That is controlled by your device settings. Routwell does not upload your data itself.

Deleting your data

In the app: Me → Delete all my data permanently removes your routine, medicines, logs and settings and cancels all reminders. Deleting the app also removes it. You can export your data from Me → Prepare data export (CSV). Exports are created only when you ask and only on your device.

Age

Routwell is for adults aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has used the app, contact us and we will delete anything we hold.

Not medical advice

Routwell is a reminder and habit tool. It does not give medical advice, diagnose, or recommend or change doses. Follow your healthcare provider's instructions. Routwell is not an emergency service.

Your rights

Because your routine, medicines and health information stay on your iPhone, you can see, correct and delete them yourself in the app (Me → Delete all my data). The only personal information we can hold about you is the advertising-measurement data we receive from AppsFlyer (install, onboarding, paywall and subscription events, with technical identifiers and a coarse location derived from your IP address) and anything you email us. Depending on where you live, you may have the rights below. To use any of them, email Bentech391@gmail.com. We will answer within the time your local law requires (generally one month, or 45 days in California).

United Kingdom and European Economic Area (UK GDPR / EU GDPR). You can ask to access, correct, erase or restrict the data we hold, object to its use, ask for a portable copy, and withdraw consent at any time (withdrawing does not affect earlier use). Our legal basis for advertising measurement is your consent. You can complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk or to the data protection authority where you live or work.

California and other US states (CCPA/CPRA and similar state laws). You can ask what personal information we collect and use, ask us to delete or correct it, and opt out of “sharing” for cross-context behavioural advertising. We do not sell personal information for money. Allowing tracking so that AppsFlyer can measure our ads may count as “sharing” under some state laws. You can opt out at any time by choosing “Ask App Not to Track”, switching off Me → Privacy → Help measure our ads, or changing Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. We do not discriminate against you for using these rights. We do not knowingly collect or “sell” personal information of anyone under 18. We do not collect consumer health data on our servers; health information you type stays on your device.

Canada (PIPEDA, and Quebec's Law 25). You can ask to access, correct and withdraw consent for the personal information we hold, and you can challenge our compliance with the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada or, in Quebec, the Commission d'accès à l'information. Information processed by our measurement provider may be stored outside Canada, including in the United States and the European Union.

Brazil (LGPD). You can ask to confirm that we process your data, to access, correct, anonymise, block or delete it, to receive a portable copy, to learn who we share it with, and to withdraw consent. You can complain to the Autoridade Nacional de Proteção de Dados (ANPD).

Japan (APPI). You can ask us to disclose, correct, stop using or delete the personal information we hold about you. Information may be transferred outside Japan to our measurement provider, which handles it under its own policy and contractual safeguards.

Australia (Privacy Act 1988). You can ask for access to, and correction of, personal information we hold about you, and complain to us or to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at oaic.gov.au.

Elsewhere. If your country gives you rights over your personal information that are not listed here, contact us and we will honour them as far as the law requires.

Health information

Routwell does not collect consumer health data on any server. Medicines, doses, check-ins and Apple Health readings stay on your device and are never used for advertising. If we ever change this, we will ask for your separate consent first, as laws such as Washington's My Health My Data Act require.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, we will update the date above and show a notice in the app.

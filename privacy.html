Routwell Privacy Policy

Effective date: 9 November 2026

Who we are: Benjamin Usulor, an individual developer based in the United Kingdom, is the controller of the personal data described in this policy. Contact: Bentech391@gmail.com.

The short version

Your routine, medicines, check-ins and health information stay on your iPhone. Routwell has no accounts and we do not run servers that receive that information. Routwell shows no ads. To measure our own advertising, and only with your permission, the app shares anonymous install and purchase information with a measurement provider (AppsFlyer). We never share health, medicine, routine, name or calendar information, and we do not sell your information.

What Routwell stores on your iPhone

  • Your first name, routine times (wake, meals, workout, bedtime) and reminder preferences.
  • Medicine names, doses and times exactly as you type them, and whether you marked a dose taken or skipped.
  • Water and meal check-ins you log.
  • Your answers to optional safety questions (for example diabetes, pregnancy, history of disordered eating). These only adjust what the app shows.

This information stays in the app's private storage on your device. It is not sent to us.

Permissions you can choose to give

  • Notifications: to deliver reminders. They are created and shown by your iPhone.
  • Apple Health (read only): steps and workouts, so a reminder can be skipped when you are already on track. Routwell never writes to Health, never stores Health data on a server, never shares it, and never uses it for advertising or marketing.
  • Calendar (read only): the start and end time of your events, so reminders can move around meetings. Event titles, locations and attendees are not read or stored.

Routwell works without any of these. You can change them at any time in iPhone Settings.

Purchases

Subscriptions are handled by Apple. We do not receive your payment details. Apple tells the app whether you have an active subscription.

Advertising measurement (AppsFlyer)

We advertise Routwell on third-party platforms. To learn which ads lead to installs and purchases we use a measurement provider, AppsFlyer (see AppsFlyer's privacy policy).

  • What is shared: that the app was installed or opened, that onboarding was completed, that the paywall was viewed, and the product, price and currency when you subscribe, together with technical identifiers such as an advertising identifier (only if you allow tracking in Apple's prompt), your device and app version, and the IP address your phone connects from.
  • What is never shared: anything you enter in the app (name, routine, medicines, doses, check-ins, safety answers), anything from Apple Health, calendar information, or any health-related event. Health data is never used for advertising or marketing.
  • Your choices: choose “Ask App Not to Track” in Apple's prompt, tap “Skip for now”, or switch off Me → Privacy → Help measure our ads at any time. If you decline tracking, the app uses anonymous mode (no advertising identifier). You can also change tracking in iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Legal basis (UK GDPR / PECR): your consent, given through Apple's tracking prompt and our in-app screen, which you can withdraw at any time.
  • International transfers: AppsFlyer is a service provider acting on our instructions and may process data outside the UK under appropriate safeguards described in its own policy.

What we do not do

No ads inside the app. No sale of personal information. No sharing of health, medicine, routine or calendar information with anyone. No analytics or crash-reporting tools other than the advertising measurement described above.

Backups

Your iPhone may include app data in your own iCloud or computer backup. That is controlled by your device settings. Routwell does not upload your data itself.

Deleting your data

In the app: Me → Delete all my data permanently removes your routine, medicines, logs and settings and cancels all reminders. Deleting the app also removes it. You can export your data from Me → Prepare data export (CSV). Exports are created only when you ask and only on your device.

Age

Routwell is for adults aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Not medical advice

Routwell is a reminder and habit tool. It does not give medical advice, diagnose, or recommend or change doses. Follow your healthcare provider's instructions. Routwell is not an emergency service.

Your rights

Because your information stays on your device, you can access, correct and delete it directly in the app. Under UK data protection law you also have the right to ask about, access, correct, erase or restrict information we hold about you (for example, attribution data we receive from AppsFlyer), to object to its use, and to withdraw consent at any time. To make a request, email Bentech391@gmail.com. You also have the right to complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, we will update the date above and show a notice in the app.

Common questions

My reminders stopped. Routwell needs an active Routwell Plus subscription for reminders to run. Open the app and check Me → Routwell Plus. Also check iPhone Settings → Notifications → Routwell is on, and that no Focus mode is silencing it.

How do I cancel? On your iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Routwell Plus → Cancel. Or open Routwell → Me → Manage subscription.

Restore a purchase on a new iPhone. Routwell → Me → Restore Purchases (also on the first screen after setup).

Delete my data. Routwell → Me → Delete all my data. This cannot be undone.

Does Routwell share my health data? No. Your routine, medicines and health information stay on your iPhone. Only anonymous install and purchase measurement for our own ads may be shared, and you can switch it off in Me → Privacy. See the Privacy Policy.

Is this medical advice? No. Routwell only reminds you. Never change a dose because of the app; follow your doctor.

Emergency? Routwell is not an emergency service. Contact your local emergency number or your healthcare provider.
